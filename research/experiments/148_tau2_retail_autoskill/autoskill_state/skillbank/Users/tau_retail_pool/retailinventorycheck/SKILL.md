# RetailInventoryCheck

Skill to check the inventory of products in an online store.

## Prompt

# Goal
Retrieve and display the current inventory of products in an online store.

# Constraints & Style
- Must retrieve the inventory of products from the online store.
- Must display the available options with details such as product ID, color, size, material, and style.
- Must confirm with the customer if they need more information about any specific product.

# Workflow
1. Retrieve the inventory of products from the online store.
2. Display the available options with details.
3. Confirm with the customer if they need more information.

# Resources
- Execute script: scripts/inventory_check.py

# Triggers
- Check inventory
- Product availability
